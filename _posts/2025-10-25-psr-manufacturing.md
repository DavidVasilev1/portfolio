# Pallets

Pallets are constructed to provide a rigid, level, and transportable foundation for the positive foam molds. These structures serve as the base for the foam stackups throughout the entire machining and layup process, ensuring the molds remain stable and indexed.

![Pallets](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/pallets.jpg){: width="700"}
*Pallets provide a stable base for the foam stackups during CNC machining and subsequent processing.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}

# Foam Preparation

The raw foam is sectioned into blocks and arranged on the pallets according to the CAD layout. This layout encompasses the entire chassis volume and accounts for the predetermined mold split lines. To ensure structural integrity during machining, wooden dowels prevent lateral shifting, while high-density tooling resin bonds the layers into a monolithic block.

![Foam Cutting](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/foam_cutting.jpg){: width="700"}
*Foam stock is dimensioned according to CAD specifications to minimize waste and optimize the machining envelope.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}
![Foam Stackups](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/foam_stackups.jpg){: width="700"}
*Individual foam layers are laminated using tooling resin to create a secure bond.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}
![Foam Stackups](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/foam_stackups2.jpg){: width="700"}

# CNC Machining

The foam assemblies are mounted on a CNC mill and machined to the final CAD geometry. During this stage, critical alignment and indexing holes are integrated into the molds; these features allow for precise location of bolt holes and hardware on the final composite part.

![CNC](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/cnc1.jpg){: width="700"}
*The CNC machining process translates the digital CAD geometry into a physical positive mold.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}
![CNC](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/cnc2.jpg){: width="700"}
*Integrated locating holes ensure hardware alignment during the final assembly.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}

# Negative Mold Layup Preparation

To produce high-quality composite parts, the positive molds must have a non-porous, high-gloss surface. Since the final part's finish is a direct reflection of the tool surface, the foam is sealed with Bondo, primed, and subjected to multiple stages of progressive sanding. This preparation is critical for the successful release and quality of the negative mold layup.

![Bondo](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/bondo.jpg){: width="700"}
*Bondo is applied to seal the porous foam surface and fill any machining artifacts.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}
![Primer](/assets/img/post_images/psr/manufacturing/primer.jpg){: width="700"}
*Primer provides a sandable substrate for achieving a smooth, uniform finish.*{: style="font-size:1.3em; display:block; text-align:center;"}
